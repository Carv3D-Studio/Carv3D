# Pricing principles

**English** · [Deutsch](PRICING.de.md)

Carv3D is intended to remain affordable, work without a subscription and keep purchased versions usable offline.

## Early beta

- The beta is free and requires no account.
- Beta builds are time limited: a free 30-day test from the first start (extendable on request); personally invited early testers can model through **31 March 2027**.
- A newer beta may extend the testing period.
- After expiry, existing projects can still be opened and saved and exported as STEP, STL or 3MF. Continuing to edit models requires a current beta or a licensed release.

## Targets for version 1.0

| Group | Current target | Intended license |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Regular users | about **€53** | lifetime license for the purchased version (personal and commercial use) |
| Verified teachers and educators | **free** | educational use |
| Students | **€10–15** | perpetual education license |

### Early supporter reward

Planning goal, not a binding promise: we intend to give a free lifetime license for version 1.0 to up to 10 early testers who actively report confirmed, reproducible bugs. What counts as "active" and "confirmed" is decided by Carv3D; there is no legal entitlement, and the terms may change before 1.0.

There is no subscription and no mandatory account planned. A purchased version should keep working offline without a license server. Future major versions may be offered as optional paid upgrades; the version already purchased remains usable.

These figures are planning targets, not a binding offer. Final prices, tax handling, eligibility rules and classroom licensing will be published before sales begin. Beta feedback will inform those details.
