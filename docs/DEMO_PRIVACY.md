## Hosting

The demo is to be hosted by GitHub Pages. Page and resource requests send technical data such as IP address, request time, resource URL, browser information and any supplied referrer to GitHub. This includes library modules, WebAssembly, fonts, examples, help videos and legal documents fetched after startup. Delivery and security of the requested preview are based on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. GitHub describes its own roles, recipients, retention and international transfers in its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The actual provider role, transfer basis and any necessary agreements must be verified before deployment; linking to that statement alone does not complete this assessment.

## Models and local storage

Geometry runs locally; the demo provides neither a model-upload service nor a project backup. Interface preferences such as language, navigation, keyboard configuration, recent commands and help state may be stored in the browser to provide the selected functions. They are not sent to us for advertising or analytics. Remove them through the browser's website-data controls to reset those settings. Projects are lost when their session is discarded. We add no advertising cookies or analytics. Browser-managed caches and technical storage may also be used by the browser or host.

## Separate services and your rights

Following links, sending feedback, filing a GitHub issue and applying for the beta are separate actions. The beta application has its own [privacy notice](https://carv3d.de/datenschutz.html). Public issues are visible to others; do not attach confidential models or personal information.

Subject to statutory conditions you have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interests and complaint to a supervisory authority. Any consent can be withdrawn for the future. Contact info@carv3d.de for our processing. Access data held by GitHub is subject to its responsibilities and retention; we cannot promise deletion from GitHub's systems ourselves.
